# Hi, I'm 沐光行客 👋

**Organizational Behavior & HRM researcher · University lecturer · Academic peer reviewer**

> Grounded knowledge · Compliance-first engineering · One product, one repository

I'm a faculty member working in organizational behavior and human-resource management, and a peer reviewer for academic journals. I also teach a course on generative-AI literacy. The repositories here are the toolkit I built for my own research, teaching and HR practice — knowledge bases that cite their sources, teaching packs with quality gates on every deliverable, and a China-market HRM bench with privacy engineering built in. I publish them as open packages so others can adapt the approach.

## 🔬 Research
- **[obhrm-brain-trust](https://github.com/Lucewalk-Lumen/obhrm-brain-trust)** — theory & scale knowledge base: 12-chapter textbook × 145 theories × 601 scales × 108 R methods, grounded citations, explicit "not covered by source" labels
- **[academic-literature-analysis-team](https://github.com/Lucewalk-Lumen/academic-literature-analysis-team)** — bibliometrics over your own library, ending in a review skeleton you can write against
- **[mplus-stats-coach](https://github.com/Lucewalk-Lumen/mplus-stats-coach)** — syntax lab, method advisor and output clinic for Mplus
- **[endnote-analysis](https://github.com/Lucewalk-Lumen/endnote-analysis)** — citation structure and review skeletons from an EndNote library
